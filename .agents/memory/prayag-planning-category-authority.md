For PTMT shared comparison codes, the Prayag planning-tab category is the effective planning category. Preserve the MRP series separately for traceability, and report any unrecognized Prayag category instead of adopting it silently. App-only codes remain outside the category override and are reported with their MRP series.

**Why:** The September comparison showed that all shared category disagreements were resolved by using the plant's planning tabs, while MRP remains identity/series evidence rather than the final production grouping.

**How to apply:** Treat the current comparison evidence as the governed source for the override. If the source is refreshed, validate shared-row counts and unmatched categories before allowing the mapping to affect plans.
