---
name: Combined operations analytics
description: Rules for presenting PTMT and Plumbing together in the Ops Dashboard.
---

Combined Management Reports must present PTMT commercial demand and Plumbing production execution side by side, never as a fabricated single total.

**Why:** PTMT report metrics are sales quantities and seasonality, while the Plumbing source is release-plan versus Sheet3 production. Summing or comparing them as one metric would misrepresent both businesses.

**How to apply:** Keep PTMT sales-history tables scoped to PTMT, use Plumbing monitoring data for Plumbing item/category execution, and label Combined cards with their respective source and unit.
