---
name: Residual confidence
description: How Pass 2 cannot-be-made quantities should be interpreted and presented.
---

Cannot-be-made totals must be decomposed into residual from high-CV categories and residual from stable or unflagged categories. A high-CV residual is low-confidence because it inherits an unreliable capacity figure; a stable-category residual is stronger evidence of a real constraint.

**Why:** An aggregate shortfall can look severe while being driven by a noisy capacity estimate, or look identical when it is concentrated in a stable category. Those cases require different operational responses.

**How to apply:** Show total residual, high-CV/low-confidence residual, and stable-or-unflagged residual in the Pass 2 plan audit. Mark each nonzero high-CV category residual explicitly as low-confidence and include its share of the total.
