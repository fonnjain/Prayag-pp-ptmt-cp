The June 2026 Plumbing workbook shows a mixed policy, not a plant-wide 1.2× drop. CPVC is 1.5× for Pipe/Fitting and 2.0× for Solvent; UPVC is 1.5× for Pipe/Fitting and 2.0× for Solvent; SWR is 1.0× Pipe, 1.2× Fitting, and 1.0× Solvent; AGRI is 1.5× Pipe/Fitting and 1.2× Solvent. The earlier reading that June represented a plant-wide Plumbing capacity correction was wrong.

**Why:** the category rows expose `BUFFER STOCK REQ FOR Jun MONTH` against `LAST 3 MONTH AVG SALE`; June's ratios differ materially by material/type, and AGRI Solvent has zero volume, so its displayed 1.2× has no demand evidence.

**How to apply:** do not describe June as a plant-wide Plumbing reduction. Treat SWR Fitting as the evidence-backed 1.2× June case; keep AGRI Solvent separately flagged as zero-volume.
