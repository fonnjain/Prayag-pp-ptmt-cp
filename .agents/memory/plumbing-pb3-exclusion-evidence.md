## Rules

- The reviewed Plumbing pending-exclusion guard accepts a source-role policy containing unmatched and resolution-loss ceilings plus an exclusion fingerprint. It does not encode a business taxonomy that distinguishes unplanned divisions from products absent from Prayag’s workbook.
- A changed non-zero exclusion fingerprint fails the guard even when the quantity remains below the reviewed ceiling. Zero excluded quantity bypasses the fingerprint comparison because the guard only compares fingerprints when unmatched plus resolution-loss quantity is above the tolerance.
