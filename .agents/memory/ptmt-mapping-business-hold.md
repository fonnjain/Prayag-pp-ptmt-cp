name: PTMT mapping business hold
description: Business-review hold for the proposed PTMT range mapping and its capacity impact.
---

The supplied range proposal maps `CONNECTION` to `Accessorise`. The current July last-month-pending capture contains 35 rate-list CONNECTION codes, but only six carry demand, totaling **63,230 pieces**: 321, 324, 325, 326, 323-K, and 324-K. The earlier 67,215 figure included seven Waste Pipe codes and is not the CONNECTION-only value.

The proposal produces a material balance concern: Accessorise carries 73,699 July pieces (45.9%) against the stated 2,594/day or 75,226/month capacity, approximately 98% utilized. Cocks Standard carries 49,228 pieces against 19,201/day or 556,829/month, approximately 9% utilized. Multiplier-adjusted demand rises from 135,800.1 to 236,379.6 (+100,579.5, +74.1%), with the increase landing in Accessorise.

**Why:** the mapping may either be a wrong business classification or expose understated historical capacity because these items were absent from prior plans or were not attributed to Accessorise.

**How to apply:** treat the mapping as provisional evidence, do not run a PTMT plan from it, and obtain Prayag’s decision on category ownership versus capacity recalibration first. The raw July reconciliation remains 168,695 source, 160,501 joined, and 8,194 unmatched; mapping must not alter those figures.
